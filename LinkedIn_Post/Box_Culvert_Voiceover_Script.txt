BOX CULVERT TOOL - VOICE-OVER SCRIPT (about 2 min 15 s, ~310 words)
=====================================================================
Read at a relaxed pace. Pause where you see [pause]. Each block says what to show first, then what to say.

BEFORE RECORDING
- STAAD open with a new blank file (kN, m), saved.
- Output folder empty (move the old Load_Calculation_Single_Cell.xlsx out).
- Crop or hide the company logo / project names in the Excel title block unless cleared.


PART 1 - THE STAAD TOOL
-----------------------

[0:00 - 0:15]  INTRO
SHOW: Blank STAAD window.
SAY:
"This is a blank STAAD file. Every single-cell box culvert starts like this: draw the frame, work out the soil springs, type in the loads. [pause] I've automated that part. Two batch files, two tools. Let me show you."

[0:15 - 0:40]  RUN.BAT - GEOMETRY
SHOW: Double-click run.bat. Console summary, then the frame in STAAD.
SAY:
"First, run.bat. Stage one builds the culvert on the member centrelines: top slab, two walls, and the base slab split at the wall faces and at equally spaced spring nodes. Sections and concrete are assigned, and the tool reads everything back from STAAD to confirm it."

[0:40 - 1:00]  SPRINGS
SHOW: Press Enter. Stiffness table in the console, supports in STAAD.
SAY:
"Enter, and stage two adds the soil springs. Stiffness is forty times factor of safety times bearing capacity, times each node's tributary length. The end springs also take the outer half of the wall. [pause] Every support is read back and matched."

[1:00 - 1:20]  LOADS
SHOW: Press Enter. Load case list; in STAAD show earth pressure and water inside the cell.
SAY:
"Stage three applies the primary loads: self weight, SIDL, earth pressure, live load surcharge on both sides and one side, braking, and water inside the cell. That's the model, ready in under a minute."


PART 2 - THE LOAD CALCULATION TOOL
----------------------------------

[1:20 - 1:45]  BUILD_LOADCALC.BAT
SHOW: Empty output folder. Double-click build_loadcalc.bat. Open the Excel and scroll: springs, both SIDL cases, earth pressure, the VERIFIED column.
SAY:
"Second tool. The folder is empty. [pause] One click, and the load calculation sheet is generated, with live formulas and the working shown for every value. Both SIDL cases are there, with the governing one marked. And this column checks each value against what went into STAAD."

[1:45 - 1:55]  WHAT IT COVERS TODAY
SHOW: Stay on the Excel summary of load cases.
SAY:
"So far, it covers these seven load cases. That's where the project stands right now."


CLOSING - WHAT'S NEXT
---------------------

[1:55 - 2:15]  NEXT STEPS
SHOW: STAAD model and Excel side by side.
SAY:

[pause] After that, I'll connect it with the P-M interaction tool I built earlier, so the same inputs give the design sheets as well, all in one tool. More soon."
